Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Effective September 7, 2026

BengaAnalytics stores account information required to operate your workspace and process service requests.

Uploaded datasets are associated with your tenant workspace and are used to provide the analytics features you request.

Authentication credentials are stored as password hashes rather than readable passwords.

Operational logs may be retained to secure, troubleshoot, and improve the service.

As the platform enters commercial operation, this policy should be reviewed by qualified legal counsel for the jurisdictions in which BengaAnalytics operates.
